## Thumbnail & icon checklist

- Icon: `icon.png` (1024×1024). Roblox scales it down, so the big title and pickaxe stay readable at small sizes.
- Thumbnail: `thumbnail.png` (1920×1080). Keep key text inside the center safe area, because Roblox overlays the play button and labels.
- The art is procedurally drawn (simple vector-style shapes, not a hand-painted illustration). Swap in a better artist's version anytime; keep the filenames and sizes.
